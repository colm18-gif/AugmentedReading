02

The one guarantee

What the chemistry proof of concept actually promises.

Where a passage is correctly identified as chemical, the structure a reader sees is derived from the passage rather than generated. It cannot be fabricated, because nothing in that path invents anything.

That guarantee is conditional on correct identification, and identification is not itself deterministic. Both halves of that sentence matter, and the second half is the one usually left out.

Chemistry is the only domain where this holds today, and it holds in a proof of concept rather than in a deployed system. It has not been hardened, scaled or run against a real catalogue. Everything else shown in the demo illustrates what the method produces, not a path with the same guarantee behind it.

03

Deliberately not here

What this page doesn't describe.

The design decisions that make the above work — how content is assessed, how outputs are selected, how the schema layer is structured, and how the failure modes are handled — are the substance of the work rather than a description of it. They are not published.

Full architectural detail, the schema design and the codebase are available under NDA. How that works
